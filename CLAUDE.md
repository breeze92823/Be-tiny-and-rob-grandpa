## The game
Co-op stealth heist. Tiny thieves (1.8 m) rob a GIANT house (x6 scale, walls 16 m, Grandpa 10.5 m). Lobby "The Hideout" (a den at `LOBBY.center` z 1500; unlike the giant house it is PLAYER-sized: `lobby.js` lays it out in design units x `LOBBY.scale`, so stalls, pads and decor fit a 1.8 m thief): walk onto a bottle-cap crew pad (3 pads, all NORMAL, 4 thieves each); the first thief starts a 20 s countdown (5 s when full / offline) -> "YOUR HEIST IS STARTING" -> the crew spawns on the sidewalk in front of the house (origin). Break in (lift the garage door, slide up a window sash, push through the kitchen pet flap, crawl through the front door's mail slot), grab loot with stretchy grabber arms, throw / carry it out of the house footprint (it flies into the van's trailer: crew bank += value), dodge Grandpa, reach the cash target (the lobby only launches NORMAL, $1,000; `DIFFICULTY` still has hard $2,000 / nightmare $3,000 for the dev `start()`) and walk into the van's glowing side door to escape. Coins = haul x difficulty mult + 60 per job done (caught = 15 %). Coins buy gadgets and upgrades (never premium currency, see the root CLAUDE.md).
- Caught: Grandpa grabs you, carries you to the bird cage in the living room. Mash Space / JUMP to wriggle free, a crewmate hits him with THROWN loot, or BREAK FREE (350 coins). Caged 45 s: a crewmate grabs the cage door to open it; then OUT (spectate / lobby / revive 500 coins). Solo + out = results.
- Noise: footsteps (sprint loud, crouch silent, Soft Socks upgrade), landings, loot impacts by material (glass/metal loudest), fast door / window / garage moves and slams, shattering fragile loot, the alarm, the toilet, the TV. Grandpa hears within `NOISE` radii (x0.55 through walls) and investigates; he ignores his own racket.
- Seen: vision cone + line of sight (walls, furniture, smoke) -> detection meter per thief (crouch, still, far, peripheral, hidden under the bed / sofa / car, Ninja upgrade all lower it) -> "?" suspicious -> "!" chase. He loses you after 4.5 s out of sight, searches, grumbles back to his routine (tea, newspaper, TV, nap, workbench, toilet, plants...).
- Gadgets (hotbar 1-9, bought in the store or on the spot): marbles / banana (slip), glue (stuck), alarm clock (he goes to smash it), smoke bomb (breaks a chase), sleep potion (thrown: naps 18 s), X-ray specs (see him + his cone through walls), energy drink, toy bazooka. Free pickups: a bazooka on the lawn, a banana in the kitchen fruit bowl.
- Jobs: 6 seeded per heist from `JOBS` (timed steal count, room / value / treasure steals, traps, thermostat, toilet, TV, secret bookshelf door, rescue).

## Architecture (`src/`)
Three.js 0.171 (vanilla) + Vite + Rapier (`@dimforge/rapier3d-compat`, wasm inlined; `main.js` awaits its init at top level, hence `build.target: 'es2022'`). Everything procedural (canvas textures, procedural WebAudio); the only imported model is the Bloxity rig `public/avatars/player.glb`. Font: Fredoka.
- `main.js`: boot (SDK -> renderer -> Rapier -> lighting, clouds, `createGame`, `initNet`, player body, avatar) and the frame (`frame(dt, elapsed)`): physics, player, avatar + `game.animate` (body poses), pose relay, remote players, camera, `game.update`, shake, render. Dev: `__game` = { player, scene, camera, teleport(x,y,z,facing), advance(seconds) (runs frames synchronously: the Browser pane throttles rAF when hidden), game (`game.debug`: start(difficulty, tutorial), money(n), coins(n), H (heist state), brain, house, props, grab, jobs, gadgets, grandpa, lobby, van), net }.
- `config.js`: every tunable. Base (identity, physics, `PLAYER_DIMS` incl. crouch, `STAMINA`, look, graphics presets, camera, settings, rig/anim) + game data: `HOUSE`, `ROOMS`, `VAN`, `HEIST_SPAWN`, `LOOT` (62 items: value, kg, material, fragile/coins/squeak/treasure), `IMPACT`, `GRAB`, `NOISE`, `GRANDPA`, `DIFFICULTY`, `GADGETS`, `GADGET_FX`, `STARTER_GADGETS`, `UPGRADES` + `upgradePrice`, `REWARDS`, `PRICES` (coins), `JOBS`, `LOBBY` (pads), `SERVER_URL`, `NET`, `LEADERBOARDS`.
- `player.js`: kinematic capsule + character controller; crouch (shorter capsule from the top, stays down without head room: `crouchLocked`), stamina, `mods` (speed / staminaMax / endless set by the game), `frozen`, `setDriver(fn)` (Grandpa's hand), `setSpawnPoint`, step / land / jump listeners. `input.js` adds `crouch` (C / Ctrl toggle), `grab` (held LMB while locked), `throw` (RMB / F), `slot` (digits), `struggle` (Space presses), `facingLock`. `touch.js`: GRAB (hold), THROW, DUCK, JUMP, RUN, CAM buttons. `camera.js`: `setFollow(fn)` for spectating; wider `minPitch` to look up at what you lift.
- `physics.js` additions: owners (collider -> loot / mover / switch / pickup), `addDynamicShape`, `removeDynamic`, `hinge`, `slider`, `castRay`, collision `GROUP`s: Grandpa never collides with jointed movers (a kinematic body pushing a jointed one against its limit blows the solver up), doors swing out of his way instead.
- `game/` (the game):
  - `game.js` orchestrator: lobby flow (pads, stalls, offline solo launch; the server answers a `joinPad` onto a running / full pad with nothing, so `padViews()` carries `mine` / `full` / `running`: the pad panel says CREW FULL / HEIST IN PROGRESS / JOINING... and `joinPad` is re-sent at most once a second until the server seats the player), heist lifecycle (`beginHeist` -> `startHeist` -> `finish` -> `leaveToLobby`), loot / noise / house events, the local thief's fate (held / caged / out / escaped, struggle, break free, revive, spectate), tutorial (yellow outline highlights), HUD feed, music moods, light pool (3 point lights moved to the nearest lamps), network sync (host snapshots `hs` 12/s: Grandpa, awake loot, moving movers, jobs, flags, the ceiling fan (angle / spin / on: the crew eases its blades to the host's, never snapping under a rider); host events `hb`; crew actions `ha`: grab / hold / drop loot, pull doors, switches, gadgets, noise, free, freed, unlockCage).
  - `house.js` shell (walls with openings, two-skin walls with per-room wallpaper, floors, ceiling, roof, chimney, porch), moving parts (hinged interior doors, inward-only front / back doors on an UNROTATED pin frame (a revolute joint on a rotated fixed frame locks the leaf solid) whose pet flap / mail-slot flap ride along kinematically while open, top-hinged garage door, a sliding sash in every window (the study's latched until the secret door opens), pet flap + mail-slot flap), yard (lawn, driveway, street, fence, trees, flower beds, climb helpers under each window), Grandpa's nav graph (A*, the study joins once the secret door opens). Static meshes are baked (`bake.js`).
  - `furniture.js` every room's furniture + colliders, loot spots (tagged: fridge, stove, tub rim, sink, safe, pedestals...), hiding spots (under the bed / sofa / car), switches (thermostat, toilet handle, the red book, toaster lever, fan cord), the toaster launcher (kitchen back counter: climb books -> stool -> counter -> butter -> drop into a slot, push the lever, POP onto the high shelf; Grandpa comes for his toast), the bedroom ceiling fan (pull the cord, grab a spinning blade = `ride` grab kind, the game drives the thief round, let go = `launch()` fling; Grandpa walks over to switch it off), the telephone (dial: it rings, Grandpa hurries over and chats ~12 s with his back to the room, sight x0.35), a light switch per room (`LIGHT_SWITCHES`; lights off = the room goes dark via `darkness.js`, Grandpa's sight of thieves in it x0.3, he walks over to switch it back on), the lived-in details layer (`buildDetails`: clocks, curtains, pictures, rugs, tyres...), the breakable TV, the fireplace, the bird cage, the safe, the fridge, the secret bookshelf door, pendant lamps, Grandpa's activities.
  - `fire.js` fire: flammable loot (paper / soft / wood / plastic) held or dropped in the fireplace hearth catches; resting burning loot lights its floor cell; per-room ~6 m cell grid grows / spreads (slower through walls) / burns loot to ash / burns out (charred) / gas can explodes; `FIRE` in config.js. Put out by the Extinguisher gadget (slot 0) or Grandpa (brain.js states `fire` = bucket runs from the nearest sink, `flee` = out the back door to the yard via nav flag `outside`; can't reach high fires or the shut study; gives up past `FIRE.giveUp` spots). Authority simulates; crew get `hs.x` (packed grid) + `hs.lf` (burning loot). Thieves in flames are thrown clear. Jumping at an open window / the open front door (from the hallway) vaults onto the sill (game.js `tryVault`).
  - `darkness.js` dark rooms: a shader patch on every lit material dims fragments inside a dark room's box (eased per room, lifted a little around the local thief); `patchDarkness(scene)` runs at start + every 2 s for new materials.
  - `props.js` loot spawning (seeded spots; loot taller than 1.15 m never takes an under-bed / under-sofa spot), a settle assist (slow wobbles damped so round-bottomed loot sleeps), convex-hull bodies, impacts (delta-v), fragile shatter, theft (outside the footprint) + flight into the trailer, authority / kinematic modes and snapshots.
  - `grab.js` the grabber arms: crosshair ray from the player's depth, the hold point stays under the crosshair at `holdDist` from the CHEST (independent of camera zoom; the wheel / pinch sets it and the camera ignores the wheel while holding). Loot is CARRIED (`carryBody`: velocity controller at the centre of mass riding on the carrier's speed, turns with the view yaw, stops short of walls, heavy loot sags / drags; its colliders drop the `GROUP.player` bit while held + 0.35 s after, so it never blocks, lifts or gets shoved by its holder). Jointed parts are PULLED (`pullBody` spring impulses at the grab point). Throw = toward the crosshair hit + `throwArc` + your running speed (`throwInherit`), and resets the loot's `lastV` so the launch is not an impact. `input.js` handles chorded mouse buttons (RMB while LMB held). Lift capacity from Strong Grip. The arm visuals (`makeArms`, also for remote players).
  - `grandpa.js` the procedural model + pose-table animation (walk / run cycle, chase, grab, carry, sit, read, sleep, slip, stuck, stunned, angry, smash...), face, bubble, stars, x-ray silhouettes, vision cone. `brain.js` the AI (states listed above), kinematic capsule, `puppet` / `takeOver` for crew clients and host migration.
  - `gadgets.js`, `jobs.js`, `state.js` (the save: coins, upgrades, gadgets, stats, tutorialDone, prefs: music / sfx / lights), `lobby.js` (the Hideout), `van.js`, `hud.js` + `hud.css`, `fx.js` (pooled sparks, puffs, debris, rings, floating text, shake, flash), `bodyPose.js` (grab arms / crouch / dangling poses), `kit.js` + `paint.js` (materials, painted surfaces, labels).
  - `models/` loot + gadget models (`makeLoot`, `makeGadget`, `makeMarble`, `makeBananaPeel`, `makeFoamRocket`) and HUD `thumbnail()` (one offscreen renderer, ~75 ms each the first time; warmed one per frame). `/models-test.html` shows them all.
  - `sfx.js` + `music.js` + `audioConfig.js`: procedural sound (73 named sounds, loops, ambience beds, 3D panning, voice budget) and adaptive music (lobby / sneak / suspicious / chase / held / results + stingers). `/audio-test.html` auditions everything.
- `net.js`: Colyseus link (saves, identify, rejoin, pose / avatar / emote presence) + `readPads()`, `serverNow()`, `onNet(type, fn)` (heistStart, hostChanged, heistOver, hs, ha, hb, leaderboard), `sendNet(type, payload)`. `reportRoom()` tells the portal which room we are in (`game.updateRoom`: `relay~room` id while the room has a free seat, '' when it is full (`NET.roomSize` = 12, the server's `ROOM_MAX_CLIENTS`) or we are not in one). Unreachable server = offline solo play; nothing is saved in the browser.
- `dev/zscan.js` (not loaded by the game): Z-fighting scanner, `(await import('/src/dev/zscan.js')).scan(__game.scene)` lists visible coplanar overlaps; `facesAt(scene, p, n)` names the pieces at a spot. Building rules it enforces: no two same-facing faces in one plane (fit pieces between each other, frames lip 4 cm into openings, decals 3+ cm proud or polygon-offset); loot/gadget models get polygon offset on pieces thinner than 3.5 cm automatically (`modelKit.finish`).
- `avatar/remotePlayers.js`: other players in your zone ("lobby" or "h<pad>"), with stretchy arms to their grab point, crouch and dangling poses.

## Running locally
- `npm run dev` (or launch config `tiny-dev` in the parent folder's `.claude/launch.json`, which sets `VITE_DEV_MODE=true`), and the backend with `npm start` there (`tiny-backend`, port 2567). Without the backend the game plays offline (solo heists).
- `npm run build` must pass. Deploy: `.github/workflows/deploy.yml` (Bloxity Hosting; set `LEGION_GAME_ID` = `be-tiny-and-rob-grandpa`).
- Controls: WASD, mouse look (click to lock), hold LMB grab, RMB / F throw, wheel = hold distance, Shift sprint, Space jump (and struggle), C / Ctrl crouch, 1-9 / 0 gadgets, J jobs, K skip tutorial, B store, U upgrades, V camera, E spectate next, L lobby (when out). R respawns in the lobby only. Never bind P.

## Bloxity
All SDK calls go through `src/bloxity.js` (never throws). Settings: `master_volume`, `graphics_quality`, `camera_sensitivity`, `show_fps`, `fullscreen`. Leaderboards (`LEADERBOARDS`: escapes, earned, besthaul, stolen) are reported by the server. Also wired (all via `bloxity.js`, each hidden / skipped when the portal lacks it): rewarded ad card in the Store (`REWARDED_AD`: coins via `state.grant`, which never counts toward the `earned` board; paid only after the server's `adReward` verification), Boxity's death screen on a caught result (the BACK TO HIDEOUT button / `L` wait for its countdown via `deathScreenRespawn()`; portal Respawn while out / in results = back to the lobby), banner ad screens (`panel`, `results`; the 3D world never gets banners), lobby buttons AVATAR (`avatar.toggleCustomizer`), FRIENDS (`social.getFriends` + `inviteFriend` + invite link) and RANKS (`leaderboard.open`), `music_volume` setting, `gameplayStart/End` around heist results, others' skin textures via `avatar.getSkinTextureUrlFor`. Play with friends (CrazyGames): the page registers `play_with_friends` after init, and `?instantMultiplayer=true` makes the first connect `client.create('world', { ..., private: true })` (`net.js` `wantsPrivateRoom`; a page opened with `?roomId=` joins that room instead, later reconnects matchmake normally); `attach` reports `<relay>~<room>` via `updateRoom` so invites land in it. Custom characters: `avatar/look.js` `buildLook` is the one builder for the local and remote looks: when `characterId` is worn it resolves `getCharacter()` / `getCharacterFor(equipped)` and loads that `glbUrl` (`loadCustomCharacter`, cached per URL, cloned per player, same bone names so the animator / emotes / body poses work unchanged, player.glb's scale, no skin / parts / proportions: `applyProportions` skips it), fits head / torso items with `characterAccessoryMatrix` (hand / shoe items are not drawn on it), and falls back to the regular avatar (never hides the player). Never sell anything for Gems (root rule): the Gems / `bux` APIs are deliberately unused. Boxity draws its toolbar + chat in the top-left: the HUD stays out of those rectangles (`bloxity.onReservedAreas` -> `--reserved-top`, `--toolbar-bottom`).

## Mobile & responsive (required for every change)
The game must play on phones and tablets (portrait and landscape) as well as desktop. Treat a feature as unfinished until it works on touch and on a small screen.
- **Input**: every action needs a touch path (GRAB is a hold button, THROW / DUCK / JUMP / RUN / CAM buttons, hotbar slots are tappable, pinch while holding = distance). Use Pointer Events, never mouse-only events. Everything feeds `inputState` in `src/input.js`. Pointer lock does not exist on touch.
- **Detection**: `matchMedia('(pointer: coarse)')` / `navigator.maxTouchPoints`, never the user agent.
- **Layout**: relative units, flexbox/grid, tap targets >= 44px, safe areas. Phones: the jobs panel starts collapsed (tap JOBS); short landscape screens put the hotbar in one row along the bottom.
- **Performance**: respect the `graphics_quality` presets; static geometry is baked; 3 shadowless point lights follow the camera; loot shares geometry/materials.
  - `bake()` / `modelKit.finish()` merge by LOOK (`merge.js`): materials that differ only by colour share one vertex-coloured copy (pixel-identical). Emissive, transparent, neon (`userData.k`) and shader-patched materials stay separate, so animate those, never a plain colour you expect to change later. Baked geometry stays indexed.
  - Rigid assemblies that move as one (doors, sashes, the secret shelf, the phone) call `compact(group)` (`bake.js`) after building: their own meshes merge per look; child groups (dial, handset) are left alone. Never `compact` a group whose individual meshes code touches afterwards.
  - Static point lights far from both zones get `userData.farCull = true` (`perf.js` turns them off past range + fog distance). Keep the visible light count equal in the house and the hideout (2 culled each) so travelling never recompiles shaders.
  - Transparent DoubleSide meshes that are flat (panes, rings) render in one pass (`singlePassFlat`, every 2 s); closed glass keeps two.
  - The lobby prewarms every loot / gadget model and its shaders in idle time (`prewarmStep` in game.js), and gadget thumbnails compile off-thread (`warmThumbnail`). Don't build models or compile shaders synchronously at heist start.
  - Loot LOD (`models/loot.js`): `makeLoot` returns a `THREE.LOD`: full model up close, a `withDetail(0.5)` build (half the curve segments, pieces < 6 % of the size dropped, no shadow) past `10 + 8 x longest side` m. New modelKit primitives must scale their segment counts with `lo()`. Physics hulls skip `userData.lodLow` meshes.
  - Room culling (`game/roomCull.js`): 2D portal walk from the camera's room through doorways / arch / secret door / windows / flap / mail slot; unreached rooms are hidden. House statics are baked per room (`bakeBy` + `classifyBox`), `live` furniture is registered per room (movers stay shared), loot + Grandpa are placed per frame (Grandpa always shows with X-ray). Hidden room contents leave the shadow pass; hidden outdoor things stay in it (layer 1, which the sun's shadow camera also renders). A new opening between rooms MUST be added to `buildPortals`, or the room behind it vanishes. `__game.game.debug.roomCull.visible` lists what is drawn.
  - Bloom runs at `BLOOM.scale` (0.5) of the screen size (`postfx.js`).
  - Dev console: never `import('/src/game/darkness.js')` and patch through it: it is a second module instance and double-patches shaders.
- **Testing**: check UI changes at 375x812 and 812x375 plus desktop.
